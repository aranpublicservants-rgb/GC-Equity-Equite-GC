Equity across the public service

Where the data agree

Government sources measure equity in different ways: who works where, what employees say in two separate surveys, and who gets hired. This page puts them side by side for every federal organization to find patterns that no single source shows. A pattern tells you where to look first. It does not prove discrimination, and it does not tell you what happened to any one person.

Sources: Treasury Board workforce data to March 31, 2025; Public Service Employee Survey, 2019 to 2024; Staffing and Non-Partisanship Survey, 2025; Public Service Commission staffing data. The comparisons are calculated by this site from the published figures. They are not Government of Canada statistics.

        Is the gap growing?

        A gap is how many percentage points worse a group’s result is than the result of a comparison group in the same organization. A positive gap means the group is worse off. Choose a group and a question to see where the gap grew, shrank or stayed about the same.
