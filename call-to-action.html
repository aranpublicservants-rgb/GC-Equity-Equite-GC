Call to Action: commitments and results by organization

In 2021, deputy heads published open letters on how they were carrying out the Clerk of the Privy Council’s Call to Action. In 2024, organizations answered a self-assessment on the forward direction of the Call to Action, including whether they had set recruitment and promotion goals. This page places each organization’s commitments and goals beside published workforce and survey data, so you can see where results changed and where gaps remain.

Letters: 2021. Self-assessments: April 2024. Workforce data: March 31, 2021, to March 31, 2025. Surveys: 2024 Public Service Employee Survey and 2025 Staffing and Non-Partisanship Survey.
