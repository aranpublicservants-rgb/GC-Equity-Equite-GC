Barriers reported by the organization
  • Operational needs greater than capacity (financial and overtasked teams).
  • Micro-organization of 30 employees with limited means.
  • Indigenous people are often bilingual but not in English/French, while positions require French and English; results cannot be broken down or reported without risking identifying individuals.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Meets or exceeds labour market availability in all employment equity categories (March 2023) and surpassed workforce availability for persons with disabilities in 2022-23.
  • In a 2023 accessibility survey, 100% of employees with disabilities felt their accommodation needs were met.
  • A 2023 Corporate Services position restricted to visible minority self-identification resulted in the appointment of a Black employee.
  • Consulted First Nations Chiefs of Police, the Association of Black Law Enforcers and Serving with Pride in investigator pool recruitment; added common objectives on Reconciliation, Anti-Racism, Equity, Accessibility and diversity and inclusion to every employee's performance agreement.

Source: Published source 1
