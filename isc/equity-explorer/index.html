ISC’s accommodation review and response

The 2026–2028 ISC–IOGC plan summarizes a Better Accommodation Project audit survey of 256 ISC employees with disabilities and 90 managers. It reports plans to redesign accommodation using the social model, including a trust-based approach for low-cost measures. A separate complete audit report was not established in this review.

Published source

The plan covers both ISC and Indian Oil and Gas Canada. Its two Indigenous Talking Circles involved public servants from across government; their findings are not an ISC-only prevalence estimate.

Commitments and employee networks

The 2024 self-assessment describes recruitment goals through 2027, annual review, retention challenges and official-language access barriers. It also discusses employee networks and language training for Indigenous employees. These are reported measures; employee outcomes must be checked separately.

Forward Direction self-assessment

Give networks a route to decisions

Proposed practice: give networks a standing leadership agenda item, a written response to each recommendation and a quarterly action review. Identify the executive who can act and report the decision back to employees.

Méthodes et limites de l’examen
  • Les résultats ministériels par groupe utilisent les lignes officielles du SAC; ils ne sont pas reconstruits à partir des secteurs.
  • Les dates et populations diffèrent selon le fichier. Ni le nombre de lignes ni un total pondéré ne constitue un effectif d’employés.
  • La collection examinée est délimitée. Un document non trouvé n’est pas une preuve d’inexistence.
  • Les recommandations sont distinctes des engagements déclarés. Ce site ne rend aucun constat de conformité juridique.
