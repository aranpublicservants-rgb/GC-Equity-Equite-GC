What each table represents

Check the organization, organizational unit, question and respondent group before interpreting a number. Survey results describe responses, not a headcount of every employee.

Department or agency
The organization whose survey results are being shown.
Branch or unit
The second organizational level reported in the 2024 source. This is not the same as the whole department.
Survey question
The exact question and topic. Compare equivalent questions only.
Employee group
The respondents to whom the result applies. Employee group categories may overlap.
Answer and percentage
Where a response percentage is published, it represents the share of respondents in the specified group who selected the listed answer, not their share of the entire workforce.
Missing or suppressed result
The source did not publish the result. Do not treat it as zero or reconstruct suppressed results.

How to compare results

Use the same question, response and comparable respondent populations. Identify the organization, branch and group. A percentage gap describes a difference; it does not alone establish its cause or a legal finding of discrimination.

Results for smaller units may be unavailable or suppressed to protect respondent confidentiality.

Source and limits

Source: 2024 Public Service Employee Survey, published “Employee Groups by Second Organizational Level” (EELEVEL2ID.csv). Detailed results are in the linked data views above.
