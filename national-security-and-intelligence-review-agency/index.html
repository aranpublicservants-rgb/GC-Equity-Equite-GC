Barriers reported by the organization
  • Only started collecting self-identification data in fall 2023, so progress cannot yet be measured; data suppressed where three or fewer employees identify with an equity-deserving group.
  • Micro-agency with very few supervisory/managerial positions and limited data to identify Indigenous, Black or racialized employees for sponsorship.
  • Lengthy security clearance process means sought-after candidates are often 'scooped up' by other organizations; recruitment from outside the public service limited.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Launched its own self-identification form in 2023-24 under a Canadian Human Rights Act section 16 special program, using Statistics Canada census terminology for comparison with population and labour market availability data.
  • Name-blind hiring piloted by the Technology team in 2022, resulting in two appointments to senior IT positions.
  • Created a dedicated position (2021) for accommodations, accessibility, diversity and inclusion as a single point of contact for equity-deserving groups.
  • Law Practitioner and Reviewers development programs require EE/D&I training and consider representation and inclusion objectives for progression.

Source: Published source 1
