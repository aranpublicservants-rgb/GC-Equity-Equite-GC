How to read this list

Some files are copies of other files in the list. They add no new data.

The four salary files from the Disproportionality Index project (employment-equity.github.io) are an independent analysis, not Government of Canada data. One of them holds independent estimates for values that the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat suppressed. These estimates do not replace suppressed values and must not be read as published figures.

Original transcriptions and data-quality notes

The original files below are kept unchanged for comparison with the cleaned analytical files. Do not treat these transcriptions as official publisher downloads.

  • Disability subgroups by department, 2024 — original CSV with source notes
  • Indigenous subgroups by department, 2025 — original CSV with source notes
  • Independent salary disproportionality index, 2017–2025 — original CSV before corrections

Data-quality warning: in the 2024 disability subgroup table, the publisher reports a mobility count of 175 for the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency, whose total workforce is 109. The analytical file leaves that cell blank rather than treating it as a valid count or zero. Disability subgroups can overlap; do not sum them to estimate distinct employees.

Treasury Board published disability table

The 2021 salary-band shares are recalculated from the independent project’s existing counts. Labels for the 2023 upper band and the 2024 and 2025 lower bands are corrected. No new counts or imputation estimates were created. The source’s 2022 racialized shares total 99.83%; this source discrepancy is retained.
