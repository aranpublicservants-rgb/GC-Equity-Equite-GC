How to interpret the catalogue

The eight October CSVs used by the new homepage are separate, small records: 2024 disability subgroups, 2025 Indigenous subgroups, 2017–2025 overall and executive employment equity series, plus independent Disproportionality Index salary analyses. They are not a complete upload of the October 27 multi-gigabyte source collection.

Older records, data with conflicting keys, suppressed cells, overlapping populations, and incomplete downloads require additional reconciliation. Public URLs to private Drive files are deliberately omitted. Keep the October 29 working explorer for existing survey and staffing features.
