How to read this list

Some files are copies of other files in the list. They add no new data.

The four salary files from the Disproportionality Index project (employment-equity.github.io) are an independent analysis, not Government of Canada data. One of them holds estimates for values that the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat suppressed.

The Call to Action page uses three more data files, built from the Privy Council Office pages of 2021 letters and 2024 self-assessments, four Treasury Board tables by department and agency, and six files of the 2025 Staffing and Non-Partisanship Survey. That page lists each source.

Sources for the Call to Action page

The Public Service Commission files in this list can be downloaded from this site. Each copy was checked against a SHA-256 checksum recorded in the list. The Staffing and Non-Partisanship Survey files link to their official source because they are too large to copy here.
