Barriers reported by the organization
  • Limited disaggregated data makes identifying gaps and measuring progress difficult.
  • Low representation of Indigenous and visible minority groups in leadership ranks; persons with disabilities under-represented in senior officer ranks.
  • Self-identification is inconsistent and the Employment Equity Act does not currently empower collection of LGBTQ2S+ self-identification data; privacy concerns limit data sharing.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Launched the Race-based Data Collection Initiative in pilot locations (Thompson, Whitehorse, Fort McMurray in January 2024; Burnaby in April 2024; Nova Scotia late 2024) to collect and report race-based data on frontline interactions.
  • Representation of Indigenous employees and visible minority groups increased among civilian employees; women remain about one in five regular members.
  • Developed an Intercultural Learning Strategy and designated an instructional designer position on the Modernizing Intercultural Learning and Engagement team for an Indigenous hire.
  • Independent review of the Officer Candidate Process to identify barriers racialized members face in promotion to Inspector.

Source: Published source 1
