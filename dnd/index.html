What data are missing

Executive and staffing data still requiring verification

As of November 22, 2026, this profile has not verified a March 31, 2025 departmental equity-group table by executive level (EX-01 to EX-05, or employer equivalent). An executive headcount is not equity-group representation. Do not infer suppressed values.

This profile does not yet establish a complete departmental applicants-to-appointments, acting and promotions series by each equity group. A staffing survey or a total marked as identifying with any equity group is not a group-specific selection rate. Check Public Service Commission source publications and request the missing aggregate cross-tabs from the organization.

These are questions for follow-up after the source review, not findings that information cannot exist elsewhere.

  1. 31 mars 2025
    L’aperçu civil d’équité précède le transfert de la Garde côtière.
