Different data, different meanings. PSES results are survey responses. FSWEP referral requests are administrative recruitment requests, not numbers of people hired. Never use either as a substitute for a department's workforce representation rate. Do not infer suppressed results or combine incompatible groups.

Official language obligations of federal offices

The first phase of the 2021 reapplication exercise, as of October 23, 2025, covers 8,750 offices: 7,978 with no change, 733 newly bilingual and 39 newly unilingual. These are office service obligations under Part IV of the Official Languages Act, not employee language proficiency, staffing requirements or equity outcomes.
