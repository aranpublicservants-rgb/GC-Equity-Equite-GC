Barriers reported by the organization
  • Balancing accountability with capacity: embedding Call to Action objectives in performance management agreements without tools and systems to make success possible leads to frustration.
  • Post-pandemic stabilization of the executive workforce slowed hiring at the executive level in 2023-24.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Exceeded agency-level workforce availability for Indigenous peoples, racialized people and persons with disabilities in fiscal year 2023-2024.
  • Created Black, Racialized and Equity Deserving Communities (BREDC) and Indigenous Recruitment Teams providing one-on-one support to candidates and hiring managers.
  • Mentorship Plus program had 97 mentees and 56 mentors registered as of May 2024, all matched, with positive feedback on career progression support.
  • Invested $759,700 in fiscal year 2023-24 to support 9 paid positions across employee networks; HR Data Hub (Power BI) has had nearly 1,200 visits since July 2023.

Source: Published source 1

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
