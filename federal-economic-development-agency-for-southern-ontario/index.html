Barriers reported by the organization
  • Small organization: hard to identify meaningful recruitment targets and development opportunities; small executive team limits capacity to recruit, promote, or sponsor bilingual EE talent.
  • Fewer qualified candidates as a regional agency; finding qualified EE candidates meeting official language profiles.
  • Creating an environment where employees feel empowered and safe to speak up (addressed via new Ombuds and resource placemat).

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • First goal attained for 3 of 4 EE groups (women, visible minorities, persons with disabilities); Indigenous representation stable, an area for improvement.
  • 50-30 Challenge: now at 60% gender parity and 25% for other EE-seeking groups.
  • 2022 sponsorship pilot to prepare Indigenous, Black, and racialized employees for EX ranks; DEI Developmental Acting Strategy with an EX-01 position for underrepresented employees.
  • CO-02 external process geared to EE groups, 50% of appointments were EE members; Student Ambassador program (September 2023) with Indigenous outreach to 5 associations.

Source: Published source 1
