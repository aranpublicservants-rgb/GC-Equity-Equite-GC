Barriers reported by the organization
  • Small organization (about 180 bilingual employees) with no resources to work full time on the Call to Action; few potential participants for programs like Mentorship Plus and Mosaic.
  • Self-identification is voluntary and only captured when payroll files are processed, so representation rates do not fully reflect the workforce.
  • Hybrid work model and Gatineau head office location complicate hiring Indigenous, Black or racialized talent living outside the National Capital Region.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Hiring managers took mandatory EEDI staffing training with the Public Service Commission (January 2023); new staffing intranet section with inclusive recruitment tools (February 2024).
  • Completed a new workforce analysis identifying gaps in designated groups' representation by job category; employment systems review began January 2024.
  • Pilot project centralizing informal job applications to attract diverse talent, with search tools flagging EE designated group membership.
  • Executive Committee members had the Call to Action forward direction commitment in 2023-24 performance agreements; new permanent senior advisor position in HR focused on diversifying the workforce.

Source: Published source 1
