British Columbia (Public Service Employee Relations Commission) v. BCGSEU — 1999 SCC 48

An apparently neutral fitness standard had an adverse impact on women. The employer failed to justify the standard as reasonably necessary.

Factors considered Job-related purpose, good faith, genuine necessity, individual alternatives and undue hardship.

Result The Court restored an arbitration award of reinstatement and lost wages and benefits.

Fraser v. Canada (Attorney General) — 2020 SCC 28

A pension-credit rule for RCMP job-sharing members disproportionately disadvantaged women, engaging Charter equality rights.

Factors considered Disparate impact, contextual evidence and whether a rule perpetuates pre-existing disadvantage.

Result The appeal was allowed; the Court found the pension rule discriminatory, with entitlement implications for buyback credit.
