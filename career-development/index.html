Choose a direction

A useful career goal can be deeper expertise, different work, leadership, or a better fit. Start with the work you want to do.

Understand groups and qualifications

Groups describe kinds of work. Qualification standards set minimum requirements. The actual job advertisement tells you what is being assessed for that position.

Sources Occupational groups and classificationsQualification standardsOfficial rates of pay

Make your learning plan specific

Use your performance discussion to agree on a skill, a learning activity, a chance to apply it and a review date. Ask what time and support are available.

Sources Performance and development plansCanada School learning catalogue

Develop leadership at your level

The Key Leadership Competencies are required for the EX qualification standard. Employees can also use them to identify development needs. Check the criteria for your own role.

Sources Key Leadership CompetenciesLeadership learning path
Questions to take to a career conversation
  • What work gives me energy, and what do I want to do more often?
  • Which job requirements can I already demonstrate with evidence?
  • What experience, support or learning would close a specific gap?

Planning suggestion: compare two or three real job advertisements. Note recurring requirements before choosing a course or asking for a development assignment.

Compare ways to move

These options have different purposes and conditions. Choose the kind of experience you need, then confirm the mechanism with human resources.

Temporary experience

Assignment

Temporary lateral work within your organization, while you keep your substantive position.

What to confirm

Duties, duration, approvals and return arrangements in a written agreement.

Source Assignments and secondments
Temporary experience

Secondment

Temporary lateral work in another eligible organization, while you keep your substantive position.

What to confirm

Home and host agreement, pay administration and responsibilities.

Source Assignments and secondments
A new position

Deployment

Transfer to another position. It cannot constitute a promotion or change term employment to indeterminate employment.

What to confirm

Employer eligibility, qualification and language requirements, equivalency and effective date.

Source Deployment conditions
Temporary experience

Acting appointment

Temporary performance of duties that constitute a promotion under the applicable rules.

What to confirm

Written offer, dates, assessment requirements and applicable pay arrangements.

Source Acting in a position
A new position

Appointment or promotion

An appointment to a position through an advertised or non-advertised process.

What to confirm

Qualifications, organizational needs, staffing authorities and conditions of employment.

Sources Find government jobsQualification standards
Temporary experience

Build Canada Exchange

Temporary assignments between the core public administration and other sectors. Formerly Interchange Canada.

What to confirm

Program eligibility, home and host agreement, duration and responsibilities.

Source Build Canada Exchange

Scope matters. The core public administration, separate agencies and Crown corporations do not all use the same staffing rules. Classification numbers alone do not establish equivalency.

Prepare an application

Keep a record of your work so you can explain your own contribution when an opportunity comes up.

Before you submit

  • Check “Who can apply”, the essential qualifications and the closing time.
  • Answer each part of the screening question with dates, context and your own actions.
  • Save a copy of the poster, your answers and the confirmation of submission.

In a pool? Ask what it means.

Confirm which qualifications were assessed, the pool’s scope and whom a manager can contact. A pool is not a job offer.

Develop your official languages

Start with the linguistic profile of the work you are interested in. Then plan practice, learning time and testing with your manager.

Read the profile

The three skills are reading, writing and oral interaction. Check the required levels in the actual job advertisement.

Source Language assessment and result validity

Plan regular practice

Use the School’s language tools and PSC self-assessments to identify what to practise. Self-assessments are not official test results.

Sources PSC language self-assessmentsOfficial languages learning path

Check your results

PSC results are generally valid for five years. Some exceptions apply, including for the position you continue to hold. Check the official guidance before a move.

Source Language assessment and result validity

Need assessment accommodation?

Tell the contact who invited you to the assessment. For language testing outside a hiring process, tell your manager. Ask early how your needs will be addressed.

Sources Request assessment accommodationSecond language evaluation

Find people to learn with

Choose a relationship that fits the question you want to work on. Ask about departmental programs and professional communities.

Mentoring

Learn from someone’s experience. Bring a specific question, agree on the time commitment and decide how you will follow up.

Coaching

Work through a goal using reflection and questions. Ask what coaching or peer coaching is available in your organization.

Career conversations

Ask someone about their work, the experience it requires and what they would suggest you learn. A short conversation can help you test a career idea.

Sources Coaching, mentoring and networkingFederal Youth NetworkGet started with GCcollab

Planning suggestion: use the Federal Youth Network for new-employee learning, GCcollab to find communities, and your department’s learning or talent team for local programs. Check each program’s eligibility.
