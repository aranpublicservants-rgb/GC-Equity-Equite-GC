Barriers reported by the organization
  • Departure rates for Indigenous, Black and visible minority employees remain a concern, especially in the executive cadre.
  • No self-identification data for 2SLGBTQIA+ employees.
  • Lack of access to full-time official language training is a barrier for Indigenous peoples, and volunteer-run networks need more administrative and financial resources.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Nine graduates of the first Deputy Minister Sponsorship Program joined the executive ranks by end of 2023; a second cohort of 15 participants began in November 2023.
  • In an employment equity-targeted staffing process in 2023-24, appointments were 37.1% visible minority, 9% Black, 3.1% Indigenous and 6.3% persons with disabilities.
  • Made the Anti-Racism Secretariat permanent in September 2022; representation improved in head of mission appointments and the executive cadre.
  • Developed the Anti-Racism and Diversity Impact Assessment Tool and dashboards.

Source: Published source 1

Sources and definitions

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
