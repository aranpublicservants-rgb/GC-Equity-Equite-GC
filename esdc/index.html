Barriers reported by the organization
  • Resource constraints and competing priorities limit second language training for EDI groups, sponsorship talent pools, and Indigenous recruitment and retention.
  • Data gaps: difficulty obtaining disaggregated data, notably for visible minority subgroups and 2SLGBTQIA+ employees, blocking a quantitative measurable targeted strategy.
  • Department size and operational complexity make organizing and ensuring accountability for training and awareness activities hard; accessibility challenges from service delivery transformation.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Indigenous Leaders EX-01 process (2019) created a pool of 43 candidates; 2021 external process for racialized employees created a pool of 360, with 61 appointed (20 at ESDC, 41 across the public service).
  • IT Apprenticeship Program for Indigenous Peoples: 77 Indigenous apprentices hired in 23 federal organizations in 2023-24 (7 at ESDC, 5 graduated); 151 hired since 2020 in 32 organizations.
  • 'From Mentorship to Sponsorship' for Black executives launched 2021 and adapted for Indigenous executives (January 2024); BEAT Collective Community Enrichment Circles supported 45 Black employees with 29 mentorships.
  • Second Official Language Learning Guidelines updated (September 2023) to prioritize EDI groups; winter session included 148 learners self-identifying as EDI group members.

Source: Published source 1

Evidence gaps

Executive and staffing data still requiring verification

As of November 22, 2026, this profile has not verified a March 31, 2025 departmental equity-group table by executive level (EX-01 to EX-05, or employer equivalent). An executive headcount is not equity-group representation. Do not infer suppressed values.

This profile does not yet establish a complete departmental applicants-to-appointments, acting and promotions series by each equity group. A staffing survey or a total marked as identifying with any equity group is not a group-specific selection rate. Check Public Service Commission source publications and request the missing aggregate cross-tabs from the organization.

These are limits of the reviewed collection, not proof that records do not exist. Military and Coast Guard sections are kept on the specialist pages where those populations apply. Employee subgroups are included in the workforce and survey sections when published.
