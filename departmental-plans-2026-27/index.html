Departmental Plans: how often six terms appear — and how much of it sits in Internal Services

Mechanical counts of anti-racism, equity, inclusion, diversity, accessibility, and reconciliation in the 2026–27 Departmental Plans of all 90 institutions that table one — each term shown as a total for the whole plan and the subset falling inside the plan’s Internal Services section, the part of the plan about running the organisation itself rather than programs and services delivered to people in Canada.

Plans cover 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027 English versions · full mechanical count completed 6 Nov 2026 Scope: the 90 institutions named on the TBS list
How to read the “internal” column — it changed definition, on purpose.

In this complete count, internal = occurrences inside the plan’s “Internal services” section only (from that heading to the next heading of the same or higher level). That is narrower than the earlier hand count of three plans, which also counted workforce and workplace passages elsewhere in the plan (key priorities, People Management). Internal figures here are therefore a lower bound on employee-context mentions — a plan can talk about its workforce outside that one section and score low here. The Office of the Auditor General’s plan has no Internal Services section at all, so its internal value is shown as “—”, not zero.

The two rarest terms

Anti-racism — 83 mentions, only 27 of 90 plans. Highest: Canadian Heritage (15), Privy Council Office (9), Canadian Human Rights Commission (6), Justice and National Defence (5 each). It is also the most internally concentrated term proportionally: 48 of its 83 mentions sit in Internal Services sections, 7 of them in PCO’s alone.

Reconciliation — 293 mentions, 66 of 90 plans. Highest: Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs (26), National Defence (22), Fisheries and Oceans (14), Canadian Heritage (13), the Impact Assessment Agency (12). Only 55 of the 293 mentions are in Internal Services sections — reconciliation is overwhelmingly program-facing. The accounting sense of the word (“reconciliation of the net cost of operations”) was excluded throughout; see the caveat in Methodology.

Methodology & caveats

How the mechanical count was done

  • One script, one method for all 90 plans (count.py): each plan’s published English text was fetched and every occurrence of the six term patterns was counted — case-insensitive, on word boundaries, in the plan’s main content, including headings, tables, and the standard Definitions section.
  • Fetching: plans were fetched directly where the host allows it. *.canada.ca hosts drop direct connections from the research environment, so those plans came via the Wayback Machine (2026 snapshots), Jina Reader, or the search-index crawler. The plans are fixed documents tabled in 2026, so an archived copy is the same content. Multi-page plans had their section pages concatenated, excluding prior-year plans and unrelated site pages; a full-plan PDF linked from an index page was preferred when identifiable.
  • Two plans were counted from their full-plan PDFs on publications.gc.ca (Official Languages; Intelligence Commissioner) because their HTML versions were incomplete.
  • Variants included: anti-racism (anti-racism, antiracism, anti-racist); equity (equity, equitable); inclusion (inclusion, inclusive, inclusivity, inclusiveness); diversity (diversity, diverse); accessibility (accessibility, accessible); reconciliation (reconciliation, reconciliatory).
  • Excluded: “equality”, “inequity”, “access” alone, acronyms (EDI / EDIA / IDEA / IDEAA — only their expanded forms count), “biodiversity”, and “diversified / diversification”.
  • Internal column = occurrences inside the plan’s “Internal services” section only — from that heading to the next heading of the same or higher level (h2/h3 preferred, h4 fallback). The internal count is a subset of the total; nothing is double-counted.
  • Verification: where two renderings of a plan were available, counts were cross-checked and corrected where they disagreed — e.g. PCO (anti-racism 10→9, equity 19→18, diversity 23→22, where one line had been double-counted), Correctional Service Canada (equity / inclusion / diversity each −2, an expansion double-count), Parks Canada internal diversity (3→2), and ±1–2 adjustments on accessibility / diversity / reconciliation for CRA, CFIA, ESDC, ECCC and FedNor.
  • NSERC correction (applied): the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council’s first capture ended before its Definitions section. A live-browser read recovered the GBA Plus definition — inclusion +1, diversity +2 — so NSERC stands at 29 total (2 / 2 / 6 / 13 / 4 / 2), internal 11, and the all-plans grand total is 4,190.

Caveats — read before using any number

  1. The internal column is a lower bound. It counts only the Internal Services section — narrower than an “all employee-context passages” definition. Workforce discussion in key priorities, highlights, or People Management passages is in the total, not the internal column. Compare internal figures across plans only on that understanding.
  2. Reconciliation and the financial sections: the accounting sense of “reconciliation” (“reconciliation of the net cost of operations”, statements of operations, planned spending) appears in nearly every plan’s financial section and was excluded — it is not the Indigenous-reconciliation term. The exclusion looks ±100 characters around each match, so a genuine mention sitting within 100 characters of the phrase “planned spending” can be wrongly excluded. One such case was caught and corrected (Polar Knowledge Canada: 8, not 7); other plans’ reconciliation counts may be low by ~1 where the same proximity occurs.
  3. Scope is 90, not 92: the TBS list page says 92 institutions but names 90; the table covers the 90 named. No names were guessed to fill the gap. Crown corporations do not table Departmental Plans and are out of scope.
  4. OCI was counted from the web version, which is abridged relative to its tabled PDF (see the cross-check above) — its mechanical total (29) understates the PDF.
  5. The Definitions section adds a small constant: the standard GBA Plus definition contains “inclusive” and “diverse / diversity”, so most plans pick up roughly inclusion +1, diversity +2 from boilerplate alone. Northern Pipeline Agency’s entire total of 3 is that block.
  6. Format differences: counts were taken from HTML main content, Jina markdown, crawler text, or PDF text depending on the source; the same plan in another rendering can differ by a mention or two, which is why cross-check corrections above were applied where a second rendering existed.
  7. The Auditor General has no Internal Services section, so its internal value is “not separated” (—), not zero. Sixteen other plans have a section but zero counted mentions inside it.

Not in scope

  • Major federal organisations that do not table a Departmental Plan report another way (corporate plans / annual reports) — e.g. Canada Post, CBC/Radio-Canada, VIA Rail, CMHC, Export Development Canada, BDC, Farm Credit Canada, and the Royal Canadian Mint.
  • French versions of the plans were not counted.
