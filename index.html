What this page includes

The summaries on this page use eight datasets: Indigenous subgroups by department (March 31, 2025), disability subgroups by department (March 31, 2024), representation and workforce availability in the core public administration (2017 to 2025), executives by EX level (2017 to 2025), and four salary datasets from the independent Disproportionality Index project (2017 to 2025), one of which holds estimates that the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat did not publish. The data explorer below adds employee survey, staffing, salary, leadership and historical data.

A difference is a reason to investigate, not proof of systemic discrimination. Do not add overlapping equity groups, compare dissimilar populations as if equivalent, or interpret suppressed values as zero.
