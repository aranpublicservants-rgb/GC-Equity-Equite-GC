Barriers reported by the organization
  • Small organization with limited managerial and executive positions; lack of advancement opportunities makes retention of EE group talent hard against larger departments.
  • Self-identification data voluntary and unreliable; some employees fear stigmatization and prejudice; data tied to pay file transfers so rarely up to date.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Employment Systems Review completed and hiring processes reviewed to be barrier-free; Diversity, Inclusion and Anti-racism Team established (May 2022).
  • D&I, anti-racism and EE commitments embedded in all executives' performance agreements, with indicators (achieve EE representation goals, bias-free postings/tools/interviews); corporate priority 'Our People' added in 2023-24.
  • Deputy head personally endorsed promotional appointments of Indigenous, Black and racialized employees; all competition posters state selection may be limited to EE designated groups.
  • Enhanced culture of awareness and transparency: employees more empowered to call out discrimination; PSES and promotion/retention data shared by EE group.

Source: Published source 1

Sources and definitions

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
  • Groups can overlap. Do not add subgroup counts or reconstruct suppressed cells.
  • The disability subgroup table is dated 2024; other subgroup or representation tables may be dated 2025. Keep their years and populations distinct.
