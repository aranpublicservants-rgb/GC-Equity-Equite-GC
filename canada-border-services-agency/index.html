Barriers reported by the organization
  • Self-identification rate about 60% vs goal of 80%; employees cite stigma, career impact and confidentiality concerns, especially frontline staff disclosing disabilities.
  • Outdated self-identification process/vocabulary; data integrity issues from timeliness of personnel information.
  • Official language requirements remain a significant barrier to career mobility, notably for Indigenous peoples, racialized employees and persons with disabilities.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Closed employment equity gaps for all categories except persons with disabilities (per TBS report); Reconciliation and Anti-Racism/EDI are 2 of 7 Agency priorities.
  • EE Executive Leadership Development Pilot (2022-23) had 11 graduates from equity-deserving groups; 4 appointed indeterminately or acting by March 31, 2024.
  • Executives mentored 21 Indigenous and 229 racialized employees and sponsored 17 Indigenous and 143 racialized employees over three years.
  • Ran 128 Indigenous-focused outreach events in 2023-24 and employs two Indigenous Career Navigators; 32.1% of language training participants in 2023-24 were racialized (vs 10.1% in 2022-23).

Source: Published source 1

Evidence gaps

Executive and staffing data still requiring verification

As of November 22, 2026, this profile has not verified a March 31, 2025 departmental equity-group table by executive level (EX-01 to EX-05, or employer equivalent). An executive headcount is not equity-group representation. Do not infer suppressed values.

This profile does not yet establish a complete departmental applicants-to-appointments, acting and promotions series by each equity group. A staffing survey or a total marked as identifying with any equity group is not a group-specific selection rate. Check Public Service Commission source publications and request the missing aggregate cross-tabs from the organization.

These are limits of the reviewed collection, not proof that records do not exist. Military and Coast Guard sections are kept on the specialist pages where those populations apply. Employee subgroups are included in the workforce and survey sections when published.
