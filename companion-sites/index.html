Equity data, departments and comparisons

Find evidence, compare published results and explore reported commitments.

  • Guided data assistant

    Answer a few questions to find relevant published evidence. Department profiles also have their own data guides.

  • Compare reported discrimination across departments and branches

    Compare published 2024 survey responses for employee groups within branches. Missing and suppressed results are not treated as zero.

  • Explore data about your department

    See published departmental representation, surveys, staffing, regional patterns and commitments together.

    Go directly to: How people are represented and leave the workforce · How pay compares · Career advancement · Experiences of safety and discrimination · Workplace accommodation · Regional opportunities

  • Understand workforce reductions and possible effects

    Compare published workforce years and examine a clearly hypothetical staffing reduction.

  • Compare public services in other countries

    Read published public-service workforce figures from five countries.

  • Track promised changes and results

    Look up Call to Action statements and distinguish reported commitments from evidence of results.

    Go directly to: Find examples of reported practices · Review responses to recommendations

  • Find a department by name

    Search the government directory by organization name and open its available data.

  • Compare teams or branches inside a department

    Select a department to open its published branch results, including employee group comparisons where available.

  • Find and download public data

    Find a dataset by subject or population. Each source has its own reporting period and scope.

  • Read or chart published survey data

    Search a smaller survey file and see published values without downloading the entire dataset. Charts are available when the data support them.
