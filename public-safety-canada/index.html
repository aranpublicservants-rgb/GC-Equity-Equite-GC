Barriers reported by the organization
  • Lack of central agency guidance, methodology or tools for setting hiring and promotion goals; workforce availability estimates outdated (2016 census) and no workforce availability benchmarks for Black and racialized sub-groups.
  • Different data sources (Phoenix vs internal HR system vs Treasury Board Secretariat/Public Service Commission) make comparing results difficult; Public Service Employee Survey questions and categories change between surveys, hindering trend measurement.
  • Official Languages policy can hinder recruitment and advancement of Indigenous peoples and those whose first language is neither French nor English; lack of integration between the Call to Action and other inclusion work (persons with disabilities, 2SLGBTQIA+).

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Closed the departmental gap for persons with disabilities in 2023-24 through two targeted recruitment campaigns, ending the year at 10.4% representation vs 9.1% workforce availability.
  • Launched Sponsorship+ in 2022 (five members of the first cohort still supported); second cohort launched February 2024 requiring EX-03 leaders to sponsor two protégés each; supports two Mosaic Leadership Development participants.
  • All executives must contribute to recruitment and retention of employment equity members in performance agreements; all employees and managers have a common mandatory work objective on wellness, equity, diversity and inclusion.
  • Built a PowerBI Organizational Health Dashboard updated quarterly and an employment equity dashboard with disaggregated data for Black and racialized sub-groups.

Source: Published source 1

Sources and definitions

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
  • Groups can overlap. Do not add subgroup counts or reconstruct suppressed cells.
