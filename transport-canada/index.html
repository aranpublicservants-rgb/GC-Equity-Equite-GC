Barriers reported by the organization
  • Many positions require specialized skills (e.g., inspectors), shrinking the candidate pool and the EE candidate pool further.
  • Amended Official Languages Act Part V (CBC/CBC for management) may make it harder to appoint Indigenous, Black, racialized people and persons with disabilities to executive and supervisory roles in bilingual regions.
  • Setting quantitative inclusion goals is hard due to lack of comparative data; exit survey data not usable due to low response rate.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Indigenous Career Navigator delivered 40+ presentations, 80+ consultation sessions, guided 25+ external Indigenous applicants and spoke with 192+ hiring managers.
  • Four advertised processes targeted EE groups with over 100 EE candidates qualifying; results shared with other departments.
  • Leadership Development Initiative third cohort launched fall 2023 with 70% EE group participants; Mentorship Plus pilot in Safety and Security Group (April 2023-March 2024); two employees selected for TBS Mosaic program.
  • Performance Measurement Framework for the 2024-2027 EDI Action Plan due early fall 2024; monthly people management dashboards and Annual Workforce Analysis Scorecards track representation.

Source: Published source 1

Sources and definitions

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
  • Groups can overlap. Do not add subgroup counts or reconstruct suppressed cells.
  • The disability subgroup table is dated 2024; other subgroup or representation tables may be dated 2025. Keep their years and populations distinct.
  • A plan describes intended work. A self-assessment describes the organization’s reported progress. Compare each with measured outcomes.
  • Only explicit source labels are matched. A predecessor, combined entity, national total or other employer cannot fill missing results.
