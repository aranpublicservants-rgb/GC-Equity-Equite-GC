CHECKS TO RECORD
- Five-question test: condition, people exposed, foreseeable harm, corroborating and contradictory evidence, decision and interim protection.
- Suggested priority (1, 2 or 3), reasons and separate legal deadlines. Act immediately in an emergency.
- Section 19.5 order: eliminate, reduce/isolate, personal protection, administrative procedures. Record reasons for options not used.
- Exclude protected EEA self-identification source records and derived person-level extracts. GBA Plus does not authorize reuse.
- Access, suppression, source, denominator, period and steward for each dataset.
- Prescribed committee participation, recommendation response and escalation of disagreement.
- Suggested closure: practice examined, controls in operation, side effects checked, reduced exposure, decision-maker and follow-up.
