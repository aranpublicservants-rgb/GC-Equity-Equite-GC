Barriers reported by the organization
  • Official language requirements as a structural barrier for EE groups (especially Indigenous and visible minority groups) and lack of available training opportunities; CBC requirement for supervisory positions.
  • Lack of data for certain groups, such as 2SLGBTQIA+ and religious minorities.
  • Integration of 1,700 Statistical Survey Operations employees (November 2023) shifted demographics; self-identification rate fell from 87.5% to 79.4%.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Sponsorship program: cohort 1 (August 2023, 12 matches with Director or Director General sponsors), cohort 2 (February 2024, 19 matches; Chief Statistician sponsoring 2); 31 matches total.
  • PE-03 recruitment process for Indigenous, Black, and racialized employees launched March 2024 (22 applicants; 0 Indigenous applicants); drop-off monitoring at all assessment milestones.
  • Executive Performance Management Scorecard with EE indicators, updated monthly; lack of progress affects an executive's final score and rating.
  • Quarterly EE Progress Report to all employees and Diversity Report to executives refreshed daily; mandatory 5-hour EDI training in all employees' performance agreements.

Source: Published source 1

Sources and definitions

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
  • Groups can overlap. Do not add subgroup counts or reconstruct suppressed cells.
