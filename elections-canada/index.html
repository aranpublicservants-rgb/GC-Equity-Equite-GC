Barriers reported by the organization
  • Small agency relying heavily (50% to 75%) on a term/temporary workforce with a high turnover rate, which makes it difficult to attract talent including employment-equity-seeking group members and to achieve representation goals.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Middle Managers Development Program launched April 2024 with a minimum participation target of 30% for employment-equity-seeking employees.
  • Built Power BI dashboards tracking candidate diversity, drop-off rates by demographic characteristic through advertised staffing stages, and demographic profiles of successful candidates.
  • All senior management performance agreements contain a specific diversity-related commitment, and the Second Corporate Commitment is assessed in senior management performance evaluations.
  • Speaker Series and information forums on diversity and inclusion, and a memorandum of understanding with the House of Commons for second language training prioritizing equity-seeking employees ready for promotion.

Source: Published source 1

Les Q44e et Q73p portent sur les effets négatifs sur la carrière et le stress.
