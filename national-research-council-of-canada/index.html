Read the original 2024 progress, barriers and employee-network account

Barriers reported by the organization
  • Consultation fatigue: equity-deserving groups report being asked too much, creating extra burden and emotional labour beyond regular job responsibilities.
  • Employment equity data gaps: reliance on self-declaration and self-identification forms and labour market availability data limited to the four designated groups; awaiting the new central self-identification form.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • Indigenous representation rose from 61% of labour market availability in fiscal year 2021-2022 to 74% in 2023-2024; persons with disabilities from 45% to 66%; racialized representation from 94% to 110%.
  • Exceeded labour market availability benchmarks for women and racialized persons overall; representation of Indigenous peoples and persons with disabilities has doubled since 2018.
  • Started a Sponsorship pilot program in summer 2023 matching senior leaders with high-potential Indigenous and racialized employees.
  • Built an Inclusive Hiring Toolkit (checklist, selection board guide, Indigenous Hiring Best Practices guide) and reports progress quarterly to senior executives.

Source: Published source 1

Sources and definitions

  • Headcounts, employment equity populations, FTEs, PSES rounded responses and staffing weighted totals are different measures.
  • X, x, *, -1, blank and 9999 follow their source definitions. Missing and suppressed cells are never zero.
  • Groups can overlap. Do not add subgroup counts or reconstruct suppressed cells.
  • The disability subgroup table is dated 2024; other subgroup or representation tables may be dated 2025. Keep their years and populations distinct.
  • A plan describes intended work. A self-assessment describes the organization’s reported progress. Compare each with measured outcomes.
  • Only explicit source labels are matched. A predecessor, combined entity, national total or other employer cannot fill missing results.
