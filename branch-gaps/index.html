2024 Public Service Employee Survey, question 65

Discrimination on the job: differences between employee groups in the same branch

Question 65 asked employees whether they had been the victim of discrimination on the job in the past 12 months. For each branch, this page compares the share who answered yes in two employee groups, such as racialized and non-racialized employees. It lists only branches where the survey published results for both groups.

How to read these results

  • Each percentage is the share of the group’s employees in the branch who answered yes to question 65, as published. The survey’s supporting documentation lists yes as answer 1 for this question and groups it as the negative answer.
  • A comparison appears only when the survey published the yes share for both groups in the branch. Results the survey did not publish, which are blank or coded 9999 in the file, are left out and never counted as zero. A published result of 0% is shown as 0%.
  • Answers is the number of employees in the group who answered the question, as published. Every answer count in the file is a multiple of five. Use the minimum answers filter to focus on results based on more answers.
  • Differences use the published percentages, which are whole numbers. A difference of one or two points can come from rounding.
  • The file does not include margins of error, and this page does not test whether a difference is statistically significant. A difference does not show its cause.
  • An employee can belong to more than one group. Do not add or average percentages across branches, groups or questions.
  • Organization, branch and group names are as published in the survey file.
