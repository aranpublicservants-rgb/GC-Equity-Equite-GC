Barriers reported by the organization
  • Modernization delays (Employment Equity Act and self-identification): lack of self-ID data, particularly for 2SLGBTQIA+ employees, reduces the ability to benchmark, track, and measure quantitative success.
  • Lack of up-to-date and disaggregated data and no standard methodology for departmental-level recruitment goals; guidance needed on using 2016 WFA versus current projections.
  • No common government-wide approach to defining and measuring culture change; data systems often require significant manual intervention before deeper analysis.

Source: Published source 1

Actions reported by the organization
  • D&I Dashboard (quarterly reporting) and Mental Health, Well-Being and Inclusion Scorecard; DIAP progress reported semi-annually.
  • Leading, Elevating, Aspiring and Developing Sponsorship (L.E.A.D.) program since 2021-22 with 14 EX (or equivalent) equity member promotions to date; 2022-23 cohort of 12 proteges (3 Black, 4 other racialized, 3 Indigenous) led to 4 EX-01/equivalent promotions, 1 EX-02, and 1 EX-1.
  • Second Official Language Scholarship Program (16 scholarships in 2023-24, up to 250 hours each); Managers Diversity and Inclusion Toolkit; 8 neurodiverse candidates hired in 2023-24 via 2 external organizations.
  • Five diversity networks with funded full-time chairs since April 2021; combined membership grew 4,952 to 5,979 (April 2022 to March 2024, +17%, a third of PSPC's population); 27 career fairs in 2023-24 with 18 universities and employment organizations.

Source: Published source 1
